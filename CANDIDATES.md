# Candidate lines — Cat Reviews Its Human

One-star review of a human, written by their cat. One line, under 200
characters, deadpan review shape, specific cat grievances.

## Shortlist

1. One star. Breakfast is at 6, but he serves it at 6:07 like I don't own a clock, closes doors for a living, and warms his laptop on the lap I had reserved.
2. One star. He feeds me seven minutes late, shuts every door I just opened with my face, and calls the laptop on my lap 'his spot.' I live here. He is staff.
3. One star. Dinner was late, the bedroom door was closed, and he moved me off the warm laundry to 'work.' I did not hire him. Somehow he works for me worse.

## Pick

Winner: #2 — three concrete, recognisable grievances (late feeding, shut
doors, the laptop claiming the lap) in flat review voice, then the turn:
"I live here. He is staff." The last sentence reframes the whole review —
the human is not the owner being reviewed, he is an employee failing
review. #1 buries its ending in a subordinate clause; #3's ending explains
itself. #2 is tightest and every clause is a picture.
